
yellowfeather manual.

introduction:
yo.
yellowfeather, or yf (pronounced yif) is a fantasy console taking massive inspiration from the 16-bit era. 
in this manual, i hope to teach you guys the ins and outs of this fantasy console; which includes the API, cassette formatting, appending, etc. 

specifications:
  - Lua 5.4 VM with 6M operations per sec
  - 512 KB Memory
  - 16-Bit Display with 128 * 96 resolution
  - 9 buttons per player (which is 2)
  - 4 PCM Channels (0 and 1 reserved for MOD)
  - 16 MB cassette slot
  - Unlimited Flashrom for saving

(NOTE: i'm not going to go over the Lua syntax for now, so please take a look at the manual: https://www.lua.org/manual/5.4/manual.html)

i haven't implemented too many customizations of the controller mappings so this will be a relatively short sections. 
the bumpers 6 and 7 are mapped to the face buttons on standard controllers, so 4 is A, 5 is B, 6 is X, and 7 is Y.
in order to player local multiplayer, you would need two controllers at the same time. im still tryna figure out a better way but this is how it is right now.
