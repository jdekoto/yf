
yellowfeather manual.

introduction:
yo.
Yellowfeather, or yf (pronounced yif) is a fantasy console taking massive inspiration from the 16-bit era. 
in this manual, i hope to teach you guys the ins and outs of this fantasy console; which includes the API, cassette formatting, appending, etc. 

specifications:
  - Lua 5.4 VM with 6M operations per sec
  - 512 KB Memory
  - 16-Bit Display with 128 * 96 resolution
  - 9 buttons per player (which is 2)
  - 4 PCM Channels (0 and 1 reserved for MOD)
  - 16 MB cassette slot
  - Unlimited Flashrom for saving
