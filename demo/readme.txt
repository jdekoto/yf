if you are reading this, then either u must got ahold of the demoscenes' source,
or found an inclusions file in a temp sandbox directory! inclusions are basically
a list of one or multiple files in config.txt that gets passed to the compiler and
is included within the rom. this does not include entire folders so you'll have to 
type its contents in by hand. still, this can lead you to have countless files that 
can aid you in your game, or silly little notes for snoopers! familiar formats like
.bin, .cm, .raw, and .maps get added automatically so you dont nessacarily worry about
those.
